# CareerPilot — User Guide

Welcome to **CareerPilot**, your personal career workspace and job-search CRM.

---

## 1. Getting Started
1. Launch CareerPilot and sign in at `http://127.0.0.1:8000/`.
2. Visit **Profile & Preferences** (in Settings) to configure:
   - Your target job roles (e.g. *Software Engineer, AI Engineer, Backend Python*).
   - Preferred work modes (*Remote, Hybrid, On-site*).
   - Expected salary/stipend range.
   - Default follow-up reminder interval (e.g. 7 days).

---

## 2. Managing Opportunities
- **Save Before Applying:** Click **+ Add Opportunity** whenever you find an interesting job opening.
- **Duplicate Prevention:** While typing the company name and title, CareerPilot automatically checks if you have previously recorded the same opening to prevent duplicate outreach.
- **Filter & Search:** Filter opportunities by category, source, priority, and approaching deadlines.

---

## 3. Tracking Applications & Pipeline
- **Track Application:** From an opportunity page or the header quick menu, click **Track Application**.
- **Resume Version Tagging:** Select which resume version (e.g. SDE vs. Data Analyst) you submitted to this employer.
- **Pipeline Kanban Board:** Navigate to **Pipeline (Kanban)** to drag application cards across all 10 stages:
  *Saved &rarr; Interested &rarr; Applied &rarr; Assessment &rarr; Technical Interview &rarr; HR Interview &rarr; Offer Received &rarr; Accepted &rarr; Rejected &rarr; Withdrawn*.
- **Automatic Audit History:** Whenever an application advances, CareerPilot records a timestamped history entry with notes.

---

## 4. Scheduling & Preparing for Interviews
- From an application detail page, click **+ Schedule Interview**.
- Enter round title (e.g., *Round 1: Coding & Data Structures*), date, time, and video meeting URL.
- **Interactive Checklist:** Open the interview detail to check off standard preparation items (researching company, testing equipment, reviewing algorithms) or add custom checklist items.
- **Interview Retrospective:** After completing the round, record the actual technical questions asked, algorithms used, and areas to improve.

---

## 5. Daily Operating Cadence ("My Day")
- Start each morning on the **My Day** screen.
- View:
  - Daily coding and career tasks.
  - Interviews scheduled in the next 48 hours.
  - Recruiter follow-ups that have become overdue.
  - Active assessments currently in progress.

---

## 6. Analytics & Reports
- **Career Analytics:** Review 10 interactive charts tracking monthly submission velocity, pipeline health, sourcing channels, and honest offer conversion percentages.
- **Reports & Exports:** Export your full application registry or interview logs as CSV or Microsoft Excel (`.xlsx`) files.
